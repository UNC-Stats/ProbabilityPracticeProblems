    Events \(A\) and \(C\) are _________.
    🔗
    • Positively Correlated
    • Correct! To determine the relationship, compare the conditional probability to the baseline probability. We know \(P(A \mid C) \approx 0.545\) and the baseline probability is \(P(A)=0.450\text{.}\)
      Because \(P(A \mid C) \gt P(A)\text{,}\) the occurrence of event \(C\) increases the likelihood of event \(A\text{.}\) Therefore, they are positively correlated.
    • Negatively Correlated
    • Incorrect. If they were negatively correlated, the conditional probability \(P(A \mid C)\) would be strictly less than the baseline probability \(P(A)\text{.}\)
    • Independent
    • Incorrect. For two events to be independent, the occurrence of one must not change the probability of the other, meaning \(P(A \mid C) = P(A)\text{.}\)
    • Not Enough Information
    • Incorrect. There is sufficient information to compute and compare \(P(A \mid C)\) and \(P(A)\text{.}\)
